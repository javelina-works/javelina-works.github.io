## What happens to your information

Your boundary and contact details are used to prepare your quote and nothing else. The analysis imagery rendered for the map is automatically deleted after about thirty days.

## From the ballpark to a flight

If you like the number, leave your contact details on the estimate page and we will reach out to schedule a [survey flight](/services/survey-work/). That flight is what turns the range into a firm quote, and everything it produces lands on the same dashboard.
