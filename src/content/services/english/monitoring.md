## What a 10 m pixel can and cannot see

A Sentinel-2 pixel is 10 m on a side. A juniper crown is 2 to 4 m, so a plant that dies outright changes its pixel by a tenth of what the crown changed. Read one treated plant alone and you see weather. Read every treated plant against every detection left untreated on the same ground and the weather cancels, because it hits both the same way. What remains is the treatment, diluted but real, and it is enough to tell us when a stand has turned and the aircraft should go back.

For a plant-by-plant answer there is no substitute for the follow-up flight at 2.5 to 5 cm. The satellite's job is to watch between visits, for free, so the flight happens when it will show something.

## Why we keep watching

Clearing brush is one of the highest-return improvements a working ranch can make, and the return compounds only if the brush stays cleared. The follow-up flight tells you whether it has, where it has not, and how much the next pass needs to cover. It is the last step of every job, not an extra.

## When to fly it

That depends on the species, the season and what you want to know. We suggest a schedule with the treatment plan, and you can change it later.
