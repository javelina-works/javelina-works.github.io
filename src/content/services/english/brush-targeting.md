## How the methods compare

A firm quote comes from the survey flight, and most firm quotes land inside the ballpark range. Price is only half the comparison, though. Here is what each method leaves behind.

| Method                          | What it treats                 | What it leaves standing                       | Soil disturbed | Chooses by species   | When brush returns                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | -------------- | -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Targeted treatment from the air | Each plant on the approved map | Grass, forbs, every tree you flag             | No             | Yes, plant by plant  | A follow-up flight finds it, treated plant by plant |
| Broadcast aerial spray          | Everything inside the polygon  | Only what is buffered out                     | No             | Weakly, by chemistry | The whole area again                                |
| Dozing or root plowing          | Everything in the blade's path | Bare ground                                   | Yes, heavily   | No                   | Resprouts from roots on some species                |
| Chaining                        | Large woody plants             | Small plants and resprouts, on turned topsoil | Yes            | No                   | Regrowth is common                                  |
| Hand crew                       | Individual plants              | Grass and trees                               | No             | Yes                  | Depends on the crew's coverage                      |

## What you receive

- A high-resolution map of the whole ranch, complimentary with every treatment
- Every target plant counted and located, labeled by species
- The approved plan, with the trees that stay marked on it
- A treatment record on the same map
- The before-picture that the follow-up flight is compared against
