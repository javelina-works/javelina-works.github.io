Ballpark embed: partner test page

A stand-in for a partner site. Paste this where the tool should go:

<div data-javelina-ballpark data-partner="brushbullet"></div>
<script src="https://javelinaworks.com/embed/ballpark.js" async></script>

This page loads the iframe from its own origin (add ?base=https://staging--javelina-works.netlify.app to point it elsewhere) and logs the messages the tool sends.
