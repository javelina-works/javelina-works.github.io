How a survey goes

  1. Step 01

    A visit

    We walk the boundaries with you, settle what is off the table, and pick where the depot goes.

  2. Step 02

    A day of flying

    The aircraft flies the ranch on a grid at centimeter resolution while the crew and ground equipment stay put.

  3. Step 03

    Detection and review

    Models find every woody plant and sort it by species. A person checks the map before you see it.

  4. Step 04

    The map, the count, and a firm quote

    You can stop here with no obligation, or carry straight on into a treatment job.
